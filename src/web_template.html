    How it works. The Game Boy is far too slow to decode MP3. This page does the work: it decodes each song, evens out the loudness, resamples it and squeezes it into 8 volume steps per channel, the most the hardware can play cleanly. The spectrum for the visualizer is calculated ahead of time too. The ROM plays that data back through the master-volume register 16384 times a second.

    Playing it. On real hardware you need a flash cart with MBC5 support (e.g. EverDrive GB, EZ-Flash Junior). In emulators, SameBoy, BGB, Emulicious and mGBA work. Some simple emulators (e.g. PyBoy) stay silent.

    Preview. The screen on the right simulates the ROM: it plays the converted audio with the Game Boy's stair-step output, so you hear the real result before you build.
